Interactive proposal · Try the controls and secondary views. Every action is simulated; no TV commands or settings changes are sent.

Less scrolling, clearer groups

The directional pad is the anchor. Volume sits beside it in a familiar vertical order. Back, Home, and Play/Pause form one row directly underneath.

Shortcuts stay immediate

All six apps remain visible. Plain app names avoid making generic symbols look like brand logos. Explicit Turn on and Sleep actions sit behind the power button.

Two secondary views

Picture comfort has its own screen, with the latest receipt and Undo pinned below the scrolling learning details. Connection contains status, recovery, and saved addresses. Both return with a labeled Remote button.

Feedback has a permanent home

Sending, success, and failure use text plus an icon. Read-only refreshes update connection status without replacing the last action receipt. Undo belongs to picture feedback; remote key presses do not claim an undo they cannot provide.
